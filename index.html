ABOUT OUR COMMITTEE

Young Star Boyz
Ganesh Puja Committee

YOUNG STAR BOYZ is a youth puja committee from Binjharpur, Bachhol. We began with a small pandal and a big dream — to celebrate Ganesh Puja together, as one family. Every year we decorate the pandal, hold daily aarti, organise bhajan and cultural programmes, and serve prasad to everyone.

For over a decade, we have been celebrating the spirit of Ganesh Chaturthi with devotion, unity and joy. Our committee is a family of enthusiastic youth, coming together to keep our traditions alive and spread positivity in our community.
